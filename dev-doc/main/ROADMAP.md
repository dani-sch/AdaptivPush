# AdaptivPush roadmap

Release order follows approved D-13. The [execution register](/dev-doc/plans/active/ADAPTIVPUSH-EXECUTION-REGISTER.md) owns precise prerequisites and independently reviewable packets; slice numbering is stable, not a mandatory serial dependency chain.

| Wave | Slices | User-visible result |
|---|---|---|
| Authority and provenance | AP-01 | Trusted catalog and evidenced migration/restore/compatibility baseline |
| Durable records | AP-02, AP-03 | Recoverable workouts and atomic program installs with preserved history |
| Complete free training | AP-04, AP-05 | Flexible relative program sequence/rest, explicit manual control, completion context, and trustworthy progression/history |
| Early paid convenience | AP-06, AP-07 | Advanced goal/customization controls and precise multiple-location equipment automation |
| Contextual coaching | AP-08, AP-09, AP-10 | Explicit readiness decisions, longitudinal/deload interpretation and optional recovery proposals |
| Unlisted distribution | AP-11 | Sanitized immutable programs, link/code previews and pinned private installations |
| Independent optional adapters | AP-12, AP-13 | Consented local health display and accessible first-party cosmetic commerce |
| Operationally gated public features | AP-14, AP-15 | Discovery/reviews and separate social interactions only with staffed moderation/support |

AP-16 account/privacy/purchase/accessibility/support/release obligations close with each relevant wave. They are not postponed until final polish. Safe parallel work includes draft/program contracts, history/schedule selectors, generation/equipment UI and independent health/theme adapters after their own gates; the register names shared-file coordination risks.

AP-01 baseline/catalog authority and the hosted AP-02/AP-03 durable packet are released. Later bounded extensions completed the workout-record boundary: completed correction and effective occurrences (`20260915190000`, `20260915210000`), scoped removals (`20260917180000`), and durable workout structure/composed future changes (`20260918160000`). AP-04 schedule database authority (`20260925190000`) is also deployed. Hosted capabilities are correction 2, removal 1, structure 1, and schedule 1; the ledger has ten supported entries and was verified September 27.

The published client adds resilient session/draft recovery and the detailed scoped Add/Swap picker. The AP-04/AP-05.1 feature branch contains unreleased dated placement, Today/Plan, schedule commands, scheduled Finish/correction, adherence, and reminders. The [flexible program-sequence decision](/dev-doc/plans/active/ADAPTIVPUSH-FLEXIBLE-PROGRAM-SEQUENCE-DECISION-2026-09-27.md) supersedes those client behaviors: the dated backend remains dormant and the client must instead implement relative sequence control, history-only ad-hoc workouts, completion context, pause handling, and the two permitted alerts. Fresh AP-04 recovery, isolated restore, schema/security, concurrency, and existing-data preservation evidence remains owned by the [September 27 production report](/dev-doc/reports/ADAPTIVPUSH-SCHEDULE-HISTORY-2026-09-25.md).

Physical iPhone/Expo acceptance remains open and user-led. Thirty-two preexisting non-rest days have no exercises, including five in active programs; they remain preserved rather than receiving invented prescriptions. The next path is flexible AP-04 sequence/timer implementation and the two-alert device acceptance, then AP-05.2 one authoritative progression policy and AP-05.3 explanations/education. Database deployment must not be misread as client distribution, active scheduling, or full AP-04/AP-05 completion. The [September 27 AP-04 evidence](/dev-doc/reports/ADAPTIVPUSH-SCHEDULE-HISTORY-2026-09-25.md) records the exact historical deployment boundary.
