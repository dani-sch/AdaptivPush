## Goal and muscle matrix interpretation

The source's D tables include strength, hypertrophy, fat loss/recomposition, muscular endurance, general fitness, athletic performance, glute/upper/lower focus, powerlifting style and beginner confidence. Strength/hypertrophy/general/mixed/focus/experience/constraints drive AP-06's initial contract. Retain fat-loss/recomp framing only as performance-maintenance context, without a diet prescription. Muscular endurance remains a retained goal with explicit rep/duration/density and recovery constraints. Athletic context can inform conservative fatigue allocation; specialized competition peaking and clinical rehabilitation require separate review and must not silently broaden launch scope. The traceability map records their disposition.

The source's E examples (two-day full-body through six-day repeated PPL) are feasible starting candidates, not an ordered ladder of user quality. Frequency never exceeds availability. A missed day in upper/lower or PPL exposes an unresolved occurrence; the solver cannot assume the user owes its volume. Time/goal conflict requires a disclosed compromise.

The F table's exact weekly set/frequency ranges for twelve muscle/region categories remain H. Preserve functional categories: glute extension/hinge/squat/abduction; quad knee-dominant work; hamstring hip and knee functions; calf standing/seated/context; chest pressing/adduction; back row/vertical pull and differentiated regions; shoulder pressing/lateral/rear; biceps varied elbow-flexion contexts; triceps pressing/extension; trunk anti-motion/flexion where appropriate; upper/lower coverage. Those categories support transparent coverage, not a mandatory exercise inventory or clinical claim. Count indirect exposure separately and label estimation rather than inflate direct hard-set totals.
